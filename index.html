SPAR Research Fellow · with Praxis Research Group · Sep 2026–Present

CoVeR: Coverage-Based Token Pruning for Multi-View 3D Reasoning in VLMs

Human Sensing Lab, CMU · Oct 2025–Aug 2026

We developed a training-free, geometry-guided token selection method for multi-view 3D reasoning with 2D VLMs. The method retains 93.5% of full-token performance with about 8% of the visual tokens, reducing LLM compute by 13.3× and speeding up inference by 2.9×.
